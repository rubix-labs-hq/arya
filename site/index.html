Media Bring your footage in.

On Mac, use Import Media or drag files into Arya, and your project file and media stay in the local folder you choose. On iPhone and iPad, add media from Files or Photos.

Edit Cut on a multitrack timeline.

Blade, ripple, roll, slip and slide on a multitrack timeline, with snapping, markers, automatic proxies, nested compositions and multicam with audio sync for up to nine angles.

Motion Keyframes, expressions and real layers.

Animate position, scale, rotation and opacity with easing presets or custom curves in the value and speed graphs. Write expressions such as wiggle(2, 30), parent layers to nulls, draw masks, apply alpha or luma track mattes and drive layers from a point or box tracker.

Color Grade with nodes, then check the scopes.

Build serial, parallel and layer-mixer graphs. Each corrector has its own lift, gamma, gain and offset wheels, curves, HSL qualifier and circle, rectangle or linear window. Load .cube LUTs, copy grades across clips and read the signal on the waveform, RGB parade, vectorscope and histogram.

Audio Mix to a loudness target.

Every track has a fader, pan and an effect rack with parametric EQ, compressor, gate, de-esser, limiter, reverb and delay. Clean dialogue with denoise and voice cleanup, measure integrated LUFS and true peak, then normalize clips to -14, -16 or -23 LUFS.

Deliver Export the file you need.

Arya reads and writes FCPXML, OpenTimelineIO, SRT and WebVTT, and exports H.264, HEVC, ProRes 422, 422 HQ and 4444, GIF, PNG sequences, WAV and AAC. What each edition exports is listed below.

Cut long recordings into editable shorts.

In the Mac app and the local browser editor

Bring Figma frames in as layers.

Import frames with a Figma personal access token. If you use the Figma MCP server in Claude Code, Claude can import frames for you. Smart Animate turns two frames into an animated transition.

  • Editable layersText, rectangles and ellipses with a solid or gradient fill, and nested frames arrive as live Arya layers.
  • Rendered imagesComplex vectors, boolean shapes, stars, polygons, lines, image fills, shadows and masked groups arrive as images you can still animate.
  • Local tokenYour Figma personal access token stays on your device. Hosted OAuth sign-in is planned for a later release.

In the Mac app and the local browser editor

Ask in plain words. Undo like any edit.

Select clips, a title or a range and describe what should change. The assistant reads your selection, playhead, transcript and rendered frames, then edits through the same timeline operations you use by hand.

  • Ordinary edits, not a black box. Every change comes with a receipt of what changed and reverses with Undo.
  • Bring your own account. Connect Claude Code, Codex or Google Antigravity through the provider’s official sign-in. Arya includes no AI credits.
  • Always optional. Every timeline, color, motion and audio tool works offline without an assistant.

Questions before you start

Can I download Arya now?

Yes. Arya 1.1.1 for Mac is on the download page with its version, requirements and SHA-256 checksum. It is free, signed, notarized and licensed under MIT. Arya 1.1.1 is also available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

What is the difference between the Mac app and the browser editor?

The Mac app and the local browser editor (run from source) support local project folders, AI assistant connections, Figma imports and local transcription. The hosted browser editor at /editor/ runs entirely in your browser storage with no AI assistant, Figma import, transcription, captions or FCPXML and OTIO interchange.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. Editing, grading, motion, audio and export all work without an assistant. Connecting your own Claude Code, Codex or Google Antigravity account is optional.

Where does my footage go?

On Mac, your project file and media stay in the local folder you choose. In the hosted browser editor, files stay in your browser profile storage. Nothing is uploaded unless you send a prompt to your connected AI provider, or the Mac app transcribes a language your Mac cannot recognize on device, which sends that audio to Apple. Read the privacy policy for details.

Is Arya really free and open source?

Yes. Arya is released under the MIT license with no subscriptions, watermarks or paid tiers. The full source code is on GitHub.
