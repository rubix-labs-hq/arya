Color

Grade with nodes, then check the scopes.

Build serial, parallel and layer-mixer graphs. Each corrector has its own wheels, curves, HSL qualifier and circle, rectangle or linear window. Load .cube LUTs, copy a grade across clips and read the result on the waveform, RGB parade, vectorscope and histogram.

Reference matching compares overall color statistics, so judge it by eye. The browser editor renders Rec. 709.

Motion

Keyframes, expressions and real layers.

Animate position, scale, rotation and opacity with easing presets or your own curve, and shape the timing in the value and speed graphs. Write expressions such as wiggle(2, 30), parent layers to nulls, draw masks, use alpha or luma track mattes and precompose. Titles and shapes stay vector layers, not baked frames.

The tracker follows a box or a point and can drive a layer, a mask or a color window. There is no planar tracker.

Audio

Mix to a loudness target.

Each track has a fader, pan and an effect rack with parametric EQ, compressor, gate, de-esser, limiter, reverb and delay. Clean dialogue with denoise and voice cleanup, measure the mix in LUFS and true peak, then normalize clips to -14, -16 or -23 LUFS.

Shorts

Cut long recordings into shorts you can still edit.

Transcribe the speech, correct any word, and ask for passages by length or topic. Each suggestion quotes the lines it comes from. Arya builds every short as its own 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9 composition with linked audio and captions in plain, boxed, word-highlight or one-word styles. Reframe with position keyframes from a tracked subject, then change anything by hand.

Suggestions rank sentence boundaries, pauses and your search terms. They do not predict views. Transcription uses whisper.cpp in the browser editor, when it is installed, and Apple speech recognition in the Mac app.

AI assistant

Direct the edit in plain words.

Select clips, a title or a range and say what should change. The assistant gets your selection and playhead, reads the project, can look at rendered frames, and edits through the same operations as the timeline.

  • Nothing hidden. Its edits are ordinary project changes with a receipt of what changed, and Undo reverses them.
  • Your own subscription. Connect Claude Code or Codex through their official sign-in. Arya includes no AI credits, and the provider's plan limits apply.
  • Optional. Manual editing and export never need an assistant.

Figma

Bring Figma frames in as layers.

Import a frame with your own Figma access token or through the Figma MCP server. Text, rectangles and ellipses with a solid or gradient fill, and nested frames, arrive as editable Arya layers and compositions. Other layers arrive as rendered images you can still move and animate. Smart Animate turns two frames into an animated transition.

  • Comes in as editable layersText, rectangles and ellipses with one solid or gradient fill, nested frames
  • Comes in as imagesVectors, boolean shapes, stars, polygons, lines, image fills, shadows, several fills, mixed-style text and masked groups
  • Not yetAuto layout, and Figma sign-in for the public release. Personal access tokens work today.

Bring your timeline with you.

Arya reads and writes FCPXML, OpenTimelineIO, SRT and WebVTT. Export H.264, HEVC, ProRes 422, 422 HQ and 4444, GIF, PNG sequences, WAV and AAC from a render queue. The browser editor exports ProRes 4444 and PNG sequences without transparency; the Mac app keeps it.

Questions before you start

Can I download Arya now?

Yes. The approved Mac release is on the download page with its version, requirements and checksum. iPhone and iPad builds are still in testing.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. Editing, grading, motion, audio and export all work without an assistant. Connecting Claude Code or Codex adds plain-language editing to the same project.

Does Arya include Claude or ChatGPT?

No. You connect your own account through the provider's official sign-in. The provider sets the plan, models and usage limits. Arya does not include provider credits.

Where does my footage go?

Arya keeps the project file and generated media in the folder you choose. A connected assistant receives the project context it needs for a request, and a Figma import downloads the design. The data guide lists each case.

What do the screenshots show?

Real captures of the Arya browser editor, development build, editing scenes from Tears of Steel, an open film from the Blender Foundation. The Mac app uses the same project format and editing operations, but its native interface does not expose every feature yet.

Is the source public?

Yes. The project page links the public source. Read its license and contributor guide before using or changing it.
