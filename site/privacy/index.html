Know what leaves your project.

Effective 2026-11-13. Arya has no telemetry or analytics. Your projects stay on your device unless you connect an external provider.

Effective date: 2026-11-13. This privacy policy applies to the Arya native apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad, the local and hosted browser editors, and this website.

Native apps and local projects

The Arya native apps store project files, imported footage, proxy media, transcripts and exported renders in local project folders. On iPhone and iPad, new projects are saved in Arya’s Projects folder in Files; you can also open project folders you choose. The local browser editor (run from source) uses folders on your Mac. Editing, grading, motion graphics, audio mixing and exporting happen on your device without uploading your files to Rubix Labs. Transcription uses Apple speech recognition in the native apps. It runs on your device when supported and otherwise sends the audio to Apple. The local browser editor uses whisper.cpp on your Mac.

No telemetry or analytics

Arya contains no telemetry, analytics, crash reporters, advertising scripts, user accounts or tracking cookies in the native apps, the browser editor or this website. Fonts and product captures are self-hosted on this domain.

Website hosting and logs

This website and the hosted browser editor are served by Vercel. When your browser loads a page or asset, Vercel receives standard HTTP request data, including your IP address and browser user agent, and keeps standard request logs under its own privacy policy. Mac installer downloads are served by GitHub Releases under GitHub's privacy policy.

The contribution form validates an amount in your browser. An explicit link opens Stripe-hosted checkout for REVRNT (Pulsar Campaigns). Stripe processes payment details under its privacy policy. Arya does not receive card details. A server endpoint verifies Stripe payment notifications and records only an event identifier and pending, paid or failed outcome. Project media is not included in these notifications. Apple processes optional in-app purchases in App Store builds under its own privacy terms.

Hosted browser editor

The hosted browser editor at /editor/ runs in your browser and stores project records in IndexedDB and imported media and rendered outputs in your browser profile's origin-private file system. Loading or using the hosted editor does not upload your project media to an Arya server.

Browser storage belongs to your local browser profile and origin. Clearing site data, deleting a browser profile, storage eviction or closing a private window removes that work. Export a .arya backup with its media to keep a copy outside browser storage. The hosted browser editor does not include the AI assistant, Figma import or transcription.

AI assistant accounts

Assistant editing is optional and runs through your own Claude Code, Codex or Google Antigravity account. When you send a prompt in a native app or the local browser editor, Arya sends your prompt and relevant project context (such as selected layer properties, titles, transcript text or rendered frames) to Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google Antigravity and the selected model provider through the official CLI or API. Google Antigravity uses a separate local Arya profile with extra AI credits disabled. Those providers' terms and privacy policies apply. On iPhone and iPad, CLI assistants run on a trusted Mac paired over HTTPS. That companion receives the request data and forwards it to the selected provider. An optional Anthropic API connection sends requests directly from the device. With Sign in with ChatGPT, available in every native app, you sign in on OpenAI's own page, and requests go directly from your device to OpenAI. Arya never sees your ChatGPT password, and its sign-in tokens stay in the Keychain on your device. Pairing tokens stay in the device’s Keychain. Before Send, Arya requires a sharing review for the disclosed recipients, destination and project data. Arya does not read or copy your CLI credentials, and optional API keys stay in the Keychain on your device.

Figma tokens

Figma import is optional and available in the Mac app and local browser editor. Your Figma personal access token stays on your device and is used only to fetch the Figma frames and assets you select for import.

Policy date and updates

This privacy policy is dated 2026-11-13. Material changes will be published on this page with an updated date and committed to the public repository.
