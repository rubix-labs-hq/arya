In your browser

The browser edition needs no install. It is tested in current Chrome and Edge; other browsers may lack the storage and video features it uses. Projects stay in that browser profile. Read the data guide before you rely on it, and download backups of projects you care about.

What you will need

  • A Mac with enough free storage for your original footage, preview media and exports.
  • An AI account only if you want assistant editing. The provider's official sign-in and plan decide what you can use.
  • A Figma account only for Figma imports. Use a personal access token until public Figma sign-in is set up.
