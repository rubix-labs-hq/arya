# Arya launch film

The 75 second launch film on the home page. It is not Arya output, so it lives apart from `assets/product`.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `launch-film.mp4` | A web copy of the film: H.264 High at 1920 by 1080, 30 fps, BT.709, AAC 160 kbps stereo, faststart. About 9.1 MB. |
| `launch-film-poster.webp` | The frame at 4.9 seconds: the year 1895 beside the After the Tide still. |

## What is in the film

- The narration is a synthetic voice made with ElevenLabs on a paid plan that allows commercial use. It is burned into the picture as it is spoken, so the film needs no captions track.
- The score and sound design are original, generated by the film's build scripts.
- The history scenes use crops of the AI-generated still from the After the Tide sample project.
- The app screens are unaltered captures of Arya 1.1.0 (build 3) on Mac, iPad and iPhone. The Mac screens come from the Mac App Store build.
- The assistant scene is one real request recorded in Arya's local browser editor, built from source, not a store build. The frames play at the pace they were captured, with only the wait for the reply cut out.
- The narration says Arya is free for Mac, on iPhone and iPad, and open source. The home page shows the film only while the Mac release, the App Store release and the public source are all configured.
- Neither the film nor the home page carries on-screen labels for any of this. That was the owner's call on 2026-11-11.

## Making the web copy

The master and its build live outside the repository, in the launch campaign folder. From the master:

```sh
ffmpeg -i arya-launch-film-1920x1080.mp4 -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 23 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p \
  -color_primaries bt709 -color_trc bt709 -colorspace bt709 -x264-params keyint=60 \
  -c:a aac -b:a 160k -movflags +faststart launch-film.mp4
ffmpeg -ss 4.9 -i arya-launch-film-1920x1080.mp4 -frames:v 1 poster.png && cwebp -q 82 poster.png -o launch-film-poster.webp
```

The web copy measures SSIM 0.99 against the master. When the film changes, replace both files and run `npm run build`, which updates their versions.
