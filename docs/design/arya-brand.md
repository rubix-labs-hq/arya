# Arya brand

## Brief

Arya is a video and motion editor for people who want to direct an AI and keep an editable project. The name replaces Reel in the product's public identity. Existing project formats, command names and compatibility identifiers need a separate migration plan.

Who visits: Editors, filmmakers, motion designers and people making their first serious video. They arrive on a desktop to understand the product, check its availability and decide whether to support it. Mobile visitors need the same facts without a miniature editor.

What they need done here: Understand how AI changes native editable layers, find an honest download status, learn how installation and account setup work, and choose whether to support development.

The first-screen question: Can I direct an AI and still edit every part of the result myself?

Tone: Precise, generous and assured.

Taste references: The user explicitly named the official DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere and Final Cut Pro websites after finding the abstract brand-led versions too generic. The new direction makes the editor and a concrete filmmaking project the main evidence. The established charcoal, ivory, amber and editorial type remain supporting identity choices.

The official [DaVinci Resolve page](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) organizes its product story around editing, color, effects and audio workspaces. The [Adobe Premiere page](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html) pairs specific editing tasks with media demonstrations. The [Final Cut Pro page](https://www.apple.com/final-cut-pro/) shows authored projects beside close views of tools and distinct device workflows. These are references for the order and weight of product evidence, not sources for copied assets, claims or page markup.

Where to be bold: Give actual Arya development captures most of the first page. Show one sample project across editing tasks. Pair a large cinematic frame with the working timeline and concrete controls. Use original media with clear provenance. Keep the cut-A sculpture as secondary identity artwork.

Never: Invented testimonials, customer counts, benchmark claims, fake payment success, dead download buttons, urgency tactics, decorative AI sparkles, private client footage, or claims about a release, platform or feature before it ships.

The remembered detail: The last ferry is a specific sample project, with a traveler on a wet quay and an approaching ferry at blue hour. Visitors see the scene and the real editor that holds its image, titles and project edits. The secondary Afterlight illustration remains available to explain independent layers.

Good means: A visitor can explain Arya's purpose, find the real release state and understand that their agent subscription is separate from the editor.

Public or private: Product capabilities and their stated limits may appear. Client media, account identities, credentials, private screenshots and private repository links must not appear.

## Position

One AI-native suite for editing, color, motion and shorts. The headline leads with the suite, and "Your edit. Every layer." stays as the secondary line.

The product promise is editable work. An agent should act on the same cuts, text, audio, paths, keyframes and compositions that a person can select and change. Do not imply that an agent can reliably finish any requested film or that every Figma feature survives import. Figma layers Arya cannot rebuild natively arrive as rendered images.

## Identity

The mark is an open A made from two angled cuts and a separate amber crossbar. The gaps suggest independent pieces of one composition. Use the solid monochrome version when color cannot print. Do not add a circle, sparkle or gradient behind it.

The wordmark joins the cut A directly to lowercase `rya`, drawn from Manrope at weight 550. The A supplies the first letter; never add a second `a` or assemble a separate symbol beside the full product name. The outlined letters share the A's baseline, with a four-unit optical gap before the r and room for the y's descender. The delivered SVG converts every glyph into paths, so it does not depend on an installed font. Keep its proportions when sizing it. Product names in prose and accessibility labels remain Arya. The minimum clear space is the height of the mark's crossbar. Do not place the mark below 20 CSS pixels in an interactive header.

Assets live under `site/assets/brand/`. The app icon uses the mark in a charcoal-green rounded square with two fine construction lines. The icon source and 1024-pixel PNG are the source artwork for native integration. Native catalog generation and installed-app acceptance are recorded separately from the website review. The website logo keeps the background clear. Favicons use the icon version.

## Voice

Explain an action and its consequence. Say `Beta access` while the request form is unconnected, `Request beta access` once it is connected, `Download Arya` once a release is approved, and `Check release status`, `Connect your assistant` and `Select a layer`. Use the same words across the website and installer. State unknown availability directly.

Speak about AI through its work: edits, selected layers, a native title and a change you can undo. Avoid anthropomorphic claims or promises of creative perfection.

The creator's appeal should be personal without pressure.

Suggested appeal: "I'm building Arya because I want creative tools that let people do the work their own way. Good editing tools take care, testing and time. I want Arya to stay accessible. If it helps you, a contribution helps me keep building it. If money is tight, keep making things."

## Pricing and contributions

Downloads are free. Contributions are optional. A contribution does not purchase beta access or an assistant subscription. Keep payment controls unavailable until a working provider is configured.
The contribution form, its $5, $15 and $30 presets and the custom amount appear only after a checkout destination is configured in `site/config.json`. Until then the support page says contributions are not open, and the footer does not link to it. Do not display contribution amounts as a required download price.

The website configuration supports `optional-support` and `paid-download` separately. A paid download needs a real checkout that returns an authorized download. Payment destinations must be registered and reviewed before publishing. The website never handles card details and must never claim that a contribution succeeded without a provider receipt.

Agent subscriptions and usage remain separate. A contribution to Arya does not include Claude, ChatGPT or another provider's subscription. Claude Code is the suggested default; Codex can use supported ChatGPT sign-in. Antigravity is not an available built-in editing adapter.

## Release honesty

Arya 1.0 for Mac is public: a free, signed and notarized DMG on GitHub Releases, and the source is public under the MIT license. iPad and iPhone builds are not on the App Store yet. Public Figma sign-in hosting remains deferred. The hosted browser editor leaves out the assistant, transcription, Figma and interchange, and pages that link to it must say so.

The site should be visually complete while displaying these limits. Unconfigured release, payment, contact and repository destinations must produce an explicit explanation instead of a broken link. A website build does not establish product release readiness.

## Directions

B: A technical feature catalog with compact screenshots and long capability descriptions. This can explain breadth but gives the work too little visual weight.

C: A product and filmmaking story. A large actual editor capture leads, with an original cinematic sample project, selectable workspace images and closer views of editing tasks. The existing identity connects those sections without replacing the product evidence.

Chosen C because the user explicitly asked for the standard of the Resolve, Premiere and Final Cut Pro product sites. The working project and its controls must be the visual evidence. Any source claim should match the capture and the implementation. A development-build screenshot remains labeled as such and does not imply a public release.

## Brand applications

The system should still be recognizable when the home-page headline is absent. Use the cut-A geometry, deliberate ivory and amber contrast, thin registration marks and generous type spacing as the common elements.

- `mark.svg` is the color mark. `mark-mono.svg` and `mark-ink.svg` provide paper and ink versions.
- `wordmark.svg` and `wordmark-ink.svg` are outlined logo lockups. Use the artwork rather than retyping the name in navigation, packaging or marketing.
- `icon.svg` and `icon.png` are the app-icon source, with a 1024-pixel raster export. Native catalogs derive from this artwork; their build and installed-app checks belong to the application acceptance record.
- `social.svg` and `social.png` provide a 1200 by 630 composition. Text is outlined, including the Newsreader accent. A public domain must be configured before social metadata can use its canonical URL.
- `installer.svg` and `installer.png` provide packaging artwork. The artwork says the release is pending. It is not an installer or evidence of signing.
- `identity-board.svg` and `identity-board.png` collect the mark, wordmark, app icon, social composition and typography in one review sheet.

`site/scripts/generate-brand.py` builds the outlined SVG assets on macOS through `outline-type.swift` and CoreText. It uses the bundled licensed fonts. Raster files are exports of those SVGs. The ordinary website build has no Python, Swift or image-library dependency.

## Website composition

The first screen introduces Arya and presents a large actual development-build capture. A workspace gallery switches among the captures that have been inspected. Do not invent a capture or leave a broken image when a workspace has not been documented.

The last ferry is original generated demo imagery. The site labels that source. Native titles, motion and cuts in the actual sample project remain separate editable elements. The site distinguishes the scene image from the editor captures and does not present generated imagery as camera footage.

The next sections explain editing, motion, color and audio through the same project. Each workspace image needs a specific caption that states what is visible. The agent section explains connected accounts and project operations. It must not fabricate a successful prompt transcript.

The Afterlight artwork appears as an optional secondary layer study. Title selection reveals Position Y. Its action moves the title 40 pixels. Motion selection reveals rotation and rotates the orbital paths 30 degrees. Scenery selection separates the composition. Every action has a text receipt and restoration. The phone view keeps the selected property visible and places a dark backing beneath the title for contrast.

Each guide has a subject-specific cover. Download shows the app-icon proof and pending release. Install shows a project folder. Support states the confirmed zero-dollar editor policy. The data guide separates local files from chosen connections. Project shows current work and future public source. Reading sections use paper backgrounds and numbered local navigation.
